The Agency Leak

Qatar property leads move fast. Most agencies still manage them in scattered chats.

Leak 01

WhatsApp Leads Get Missed

Inquiries arrive from Instagram, portals, referrals, QR codes, and signboards. If no one answers fast, the client messages another agency.

Leak 02

Client Data Lives On Phones

When conversations sit inside personal broker accounts, managers lose context, history, and sometimes the client relationship.

Leak 03

Managers Cannot See The Pipeline

It is hard to know which leads were answered, which agents are overloaded, and which conversations became viewings or offers.

Leak 04

Follow-Up Depends On Memory

Viewing reminders, new matching listings, owner updates, and no-reply follow-ups get delayed or forgotten without automation.
